Meet The Artist

Hey, I'm Jamie

I'm a full-time artist living and creating in Rockaway Beach, NY. My work is inspired by the organic and intricate beauty found in nature. I find so much inspiration in the colours, textures and overall feeling of peace of the coastline.

My process is intuitive and organic. What comes through in each piece is some combination of gathered visual inspiration from nature, remembered life experiences and the feelings that remain long after an experience has passed.

More than anything, the act of creating is a meditative experience for me. The process itself brings me peace, and I can't wait to share that with you.
